Built from 7 photographed receipts (Aug 2026). One-off / durable purchases (mops, lamps, storage boxes, bottle & can deposits) were left out — this list is the stuff that actually gets rebought. “Life (days)” is our estimate of how long one purchase lasts your household (judgment calls, not measured data); “Remaining days” = Life − days since Last purchase, so it counts down on its own as time passes — sort that column to see what needs buying soonest. Rows with a ⚠ next to the date came from the one receipt whose date/time footer wasn't captured in the photo, so that date is a placeholder. “Online now” prices and photos come from each shop's site where a confident product match was found; Lidl doesn't keep a persistent online catalogue, so its items show without one. Checkbox selections are saved in this browser only (via local storage) — they won't follow you to another device or browser. The “Selected” button's total is the sum of Last price paid across whatever's checked (falling back to Online now when a last price isn't on file).
